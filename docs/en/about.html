About us

ZeroMatrixTech is the outward-facing technology brand of 北京凌矩阵科技有限责任公司. The team has backgrounds in computer science, computational science, electronic engineering, information engineering and related fields.

The company operates with a lean, project-driven structure focused on technology development, engineering implementation, product development and business exploration. Building on computer sales and after-sales support, we are expanding into enterprise IT services, software development and technical services, with a focus on practical needs, clear deliverables and ongoing maintenance.
